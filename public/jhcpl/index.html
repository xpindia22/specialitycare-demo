About Us

Healthcare that feels personal, not transactional.

James Heart Care Pvt. Ltd. was founded with one simple idea — that good medicine starts with listening. We take time with every patient, explain what's going on, and walk you through your options without pressure.

Our clinic combines OPD consultations, diagnostic services like ECG and Echo, an in-house lab and pharmacy, so most of what you need to get well happens in a single visit.

Experienced physicians
Years of clinical practice across general medicine, cardiology & more.
In-house diagnostics
ECG, Echo and routine lab work — no running between facilities.
WhatsApp updates

Pharmacy on-site
Prescription filled before you leave; no second trip needed.
Our Services

Everything under one roof

Consultation, diagnostics, lab, pharmacy and preventive care — we've designed the clinic so that a single visit gets most of what you need done.

General Consultation

OPD consultation for fever, infections, BP, diabetes, lifestyle issues and routine health concerns.

ECG

12-lead resting electrocardiogram — same-day, read by our physicians, report on WhatsApp.

2D Echo

Non-invasive echocardiography to check heart chambers, valves and pumping function.

Lab Tests

In-house collection for CBC, sugar, lipid profile, thyroid, liver/kidney panels and more.

Pharmacy

On-site dispensing of prescribed medicines, so your treatment starts the same hour.

Vaccinations

Routine childhood and adult immunisations, with reminder schedules on WhatsApp.

Health Checkups

Annual and pre-employment checkup packages — bundled tests, single visit, single report.

Medical Certificates

Fitness certificates, sick leave, school/sports certificates — issued by registered physicians.
